About role
Location: Remote (CET +/- 2h)

The Mission

Most companies talk about AI; we ship it. At Sudolabs, we don't just build wrappers. We build LLM-based applications, agentic systems, and multimodal workflows for global clients ranging from YC startups to Fortune 500s (UNIQA, U.S. Steel, iQor).

We are looking for a high-agency Senior PM who wants to lead client transformations into new AI-agentic operating models. You'll work in small, autonomous teams (3–5 people) where the typical delivery lead time is under 12 weeks. No heavy hierarchy. No internal politics. Just building.

How You'll Work (The AI-Native Edge)

Our company culture is tech-native and AI-oriented. Our PMs don't just use ChatGPT, they run their work through our internal "Product-Harness"(agentic PM tooling), Claude Code, Figma, GitHub and any other individually preferred tools & systems.

Unlimited AI Tokens: Use whatever model, whatever budget—no questions asked.
Bleeding Edge: If a new framework drops Tuesday, you're expected to be testing it by Wednesday.
Total Leverage: We cover your tooling budget so you can build your own automations and pipelines to move faster than any traditional PM.

What You'll Own

1. Discovery & Strategy

Lead AI Discovery workshops to map and prioritize use-cases.
Validate ideas through rapid prototyping.
Shape solution architecture with engineering.
Score feasibility vs. business impact (ROI logic).
Define product roadmaps and development scopes

2. Delivery & Scale

Own the client relationship end-to-end as the primary point of contact.
Translate technical trade-offs for non-technical stakeholders.
Manage scope, timeline, and budget without breaking the relationship.
Spot growth opportunities within the account to drive account retention.

You're a fit if...

You are Technically Confident: You can discuss system architecture, estimates and data models with engineers without getting lost.
You are Delivery-Proven: You've owned real software projects with real budgets and deadlines.
You are High-Agency: You don't wait for permission to fix a process, set a direction or try a new approach.
You are Commercially Minded: You understand that clients are paying us to solve their business problems and that we need to bring them a real tangible value.
Clear Communicator: You are structured in your work as well as in your communication, you embrace Pyramid Principle and can facilitate complex product workshops.

This is probably NOT for you if...

You want a purely internal facing role with no client contact.
You prefer clear instructions and standardized playbooks.
You are passive about AI tools and not curious to test new capabilities.
You want to manage people rather than outcomes.

Why Sudolabs?

Autonomy: No 9-to-5 "clocking in." You define how and where the work gets done.
